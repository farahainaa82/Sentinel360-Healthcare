# Step 3B Risk and Alert Priority Rules

## Sort Order
1. operational escalation (contains Escalation)
2. risk_tier numeric descending
3. urgency ascending (Immediate Review first)
4. breach severity (Red/Critical first)
5. sustained deterioration
6. management attention level

## Display
- Top 5, 10, or 20 selectable
- Columns: Hospital, Department, Dominant KPI, Risk Tier, Urgency, Management Attention, Readiness, Primary Queue, Main Warning, Reporting Date
- Status badges used

## Constraints
- Financial value does not determine primary rank
- Operational urgency takes precedence
