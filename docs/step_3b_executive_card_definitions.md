# Step 3B Executive Card Definitions

## Card 1 — Packages Requiring Attention
- Definition: management_attention_level in (Immediate Management Attention, Priority Management Review)
- Colour rule: red

## Card 2 — Critical and High Risk
- Definition: risk_tier >= 70
- Colour rule: red

## Card 3 — Ready for Management Review
- Definition: readiness_status = Ready for Integrated Management Review
- Colour rule: green

## Card 4 — Ready with Conditions
- Definition: readiness_status = Ready with Conditions
- Colour rule: amber

## Card 5 — Monitoring Only
- Definition: readiness_status = Monitoring Only
- Colour rule: green

## Card 6 — Pending Management Review
- Definition: approval_status = Pending Management Review
- Colour rule: amber

Each card displays count and percentage of the filtered population.
